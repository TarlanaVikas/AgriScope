About

How AgriScope reads a leaf.

AgriScope is a web interface around a trained YOLO object-detection model. It is built to make the model’s raw output easy to read and to be clear about what that output does and does not mean.

The model

TaskObject detection: finds boxes in an image and assigns each a class and a confidence score.

Reading a result

  • Confidence is the model’s own score for a box. It is not a probability that the diagnosis is correct, and it can be high for a wrong class.
  • Threshold controls which boxes are shown. Lower it to see weaker guesses; raise it to keep only strong ones.
  • No detection means nothing matched at that threshold. It does not prove a plant is healthy.
  • Guidance text (signs and next steps) is static, general reference material written for each class. It is not generated by the model and is not a diagnosis.

Privacy

Photos you upload are read into memory, analyzed, and discarded when the request ends. AgriScope does not write them to disk, keep a history, or use filenames. If you host AgriScope behind a proxy or platform, that layer may keep its own logs.

Limitations

  • The model only knows its trained classes and cannot identify the crop species.
  • Photos with several leaves, cluttered backgrounds, poor light or heavy blur are harder.
  • Similar-looking problems (for example several viral or nutrient issues) can be confused.
  • For decisions about treatment or sale of a crop, confirm with a local agricultural adviser or a laboratory.
