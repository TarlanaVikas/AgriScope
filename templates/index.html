How it works

From a leaf photo to a reading you can act on.

  1. 1

    Upload a photo

    Drop in a JPG, PNG or WebP of a single leaf, or pick one of the sample photos. The file is checked before anything is analyzed.

  2. 2

    Analyze with YOLO

    A YOLO object detector scans the image and proposes boxes around regions that match the classes it was trained on, each with a confidence score.

  3. 3

    Review the findings

    See every detection drawn on your photo, grouped by class, with plain-language notes on what the signs usually mean and general next steps.

Straight talk

Useful, but not a laboratory.

One photo, one opinion

A detector sees pixels, not the field. Lighting, focus, leaf age and background all change what it reports.

Only what it was trained on

It will not tell you which crop it is looking at, and it can confuse look-alike symptoms. Treat low confidence as a hint, not a verdict.

No published accuracy

The training data and evaluation results for the bundled weights were not provided, so AgriScope does not quote an accuracy figure.
